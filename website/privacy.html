Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

Trackly is a habit tracker made by an independent developer. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app. In short: your habits stay on your phone.

Summary

  • No account and no sign-up.
  • No advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your habits, history, reminders and settings are stored only on your device.
  • We do not collect, sell or share your personal data.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the habits you create, the progress you log, your reminders and your settings in its private storage on your device. This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. Other apps cannot read it.

Android backup

If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include the app's data in your device backup in your Google account, so it can be restored on a new phone. This backup is managed by Google under its own privacy policy, and you can turn it off in Android settings.

Your own backups and exports

If you use "Back up to a file" or "Export to CSV", the app writes a file to the place you choose, for example your phone or a cloud drive. The file is handled by that storage provider. We never receive it.

Purchases

Trackly Premium is sold through Google Play. Payments are processed by Google; we never receive your card or payment details. The app asks Google Play only whether you own Premium. Google's privacy policy applies to purchases: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Ratings

The app may show Google Play's rating dialog. Ratings and reviews go to Google Play, not to us.

Permissions

  • Notifications — to show the habit reminders you set.
  • Run at startup — to restore your reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration — for reminder vibration.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from them.

Deleting your data

You can delete a habit and its history in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all data stored by the app. Backup files you created stay where you saved them until you delete them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in the app and on the app's website with a new effective date.

Contact

You can contact the developer through the e-mail address shown on Trackly's page in Google Play.

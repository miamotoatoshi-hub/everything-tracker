Questions

Is Trackly free?

Yes. You can track up to 5 habits with reminders and weekly statistics for free, forever. Premium is optional.

Do I need an account?

No. Trackly works without an account and without the internet.

What happens to my data if I change phones?

Use Settings ▸ Your data ▸ Back up to a file, then restore the file on the new phone. Android's own device backup can also bring it over.

How do I cancel Premium?

In Google Play: Payments & subscriptions ▸ Subscriptions ▸ Trackly. Premium stays active until the end of the paid period.

Which languages are supported?

English, Русский, Español, Deutsch, Français and Português (Brasil).

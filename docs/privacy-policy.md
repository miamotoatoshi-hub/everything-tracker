Trackly ("the app") is a habit tracker. This policy explains what data the app handles.

## Data stored on your device
Your habits, progress, reminders and settings are stored **only on your device**. The developer has no server and
cannot see, collect or sell this data. There are no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics in the app.

## Backups and exports
If you use *Back up to a file* or *Export to CSV*, the app writes a file to the location **you** choose (for
example your device storage or a cloud drive such as Google Drive). That file is handled by the storage
provider you chose, under its own privacy policy. The developer never receives it.

## Purchases
Premium is sold through **Google Play Billing**. Payments are processed by Google; the developer does not receive
your payment details. The app asks Google Play whether you own Premium. See Google's privacy policy:
<https://policies.google.com/privacy>.

## Ratings
The app may show Google Play's in-app review dialog. Ratings and reviews are submitted to Google Play, not to the
developer's server.

## Notifications
Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. You can turn them off in the app or in Android settings.

## Permissions
* **Notifications** — to show habit reminders.
* **Run at startup** — to restore reminders after the phone restarts.
* **Vibration** — for reminder vibration.

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13.

## Deleting your data
Uninstalling the app (or *Settings ▸ Apps ▸ Trackly ▸ Clear storage*) deletes all data stored by the app.
Backup files you created stay where you saved them until you delete them.
